Bach: Procedural Music Generator

DawgHack 2025 · Team of three

Bach composes short, multi-instrument MIDI pieces from a few choices (tempo, mood, instruments, and how "zany" it should be) without any machine learning. Everything comes from music theory and probability.

How it works

  • Each of seven moods maps to one of the seven Greek modes, from Ionian to Locrian, with its own rhythmic feel.
  • A first-order Markov chain picks chord progressions, with separate major and minor transition tables that follow common harmonic movement.
  • Chord, bass, and lead tracks are generated separately and merged into one MIDI file.
  • Random 7ths, 9ths, octave shifts, and an adjustable chance of altered chords keep pieces from sounding mechanical.

My role: Front end and integration

  • Built the Streamlit web interface and its parameter inputs.
  • Connected those inputs to the composition engine.
  • Added in-browser playback and one-click .mid download.
