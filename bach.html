Try Bach

Pick a tempo, mood, and instruments, and Bach composes a short multi-instrument piece using music theory and probability. No AI.

How to use it

  1. Choose a Tempo and Mood.
  2. Pick instruments for the Chord, Bass, and Lead.
  3. Slide Jazz and Zany up for richer, stranger harmony.
  4. Click Create, or Randomize to let Bach choose.
  5. Press play on the player, or Download the .mid file.

The demo runs on Streamlit's free hosting. If it's been idle it may show a sleep screen; click the button to wake it and give it about 30 seconds.
